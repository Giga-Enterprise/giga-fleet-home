Operations Core

Dispatch built for the complexity of commercial waste.

Commercial waste isn't residential. You're managing contracts, territories, exceptions, sub-haulers, and compliance obligations simultaneously. GigaFleet's operations core is purpose-built for this complexity.

  • Auto-routing
    Optimize multi-stop routes automatically with constraint-aware scheduling.
  • Floating Tasks
    Unscheduled work items that get absorbed into routes as capacity allows.
  • Work Orders
    Full job lifecycle tracking from dispatch to completion and billing.
  • Geo-fencing
    Virtual perimeters with automatic entry/exit events and alerts.
  • Territory Management
    Define, visualize, and enforce service territories by contract.
  • Sub-Organizations
    Manage subsidiary haulers and contractors within one platform.
  • Scheduling & Calendar
    Forward-looking schedule management with conflict detection.
Field & Driver

The driver surface, reinvented.

Works with zero signal — always

No signal? Not a problem. GigaFleet's mobile suite works completely offline — tasks, photos, forms, and signatures all queue locally and sync in order when connectivity returns.

  • GPS-Stamped Photos
    Coordinates, timestamp, battery, driver ID, and device ID burned into every photo.
  • Cryptographic Signatures
    Delivery and inspection proof that can't be forged or disputed.
  • Full Telemetry
    GPS accuracy radius, heading, altitude, and battery level — not just a dot on a map.
  • Watermarked Evidence
    Every photo is legally defensible with embedded metadata — immutable and chain-of-custody ready.
  • Driver Console
    Purpose-built tablet display for the cab with real-time route and telemetry.
  • Offline-First
    Complete functionality with zero signal. Nothing waits for connectivity — queue and sync, in order.
  • Real-Time Tracker
    Live vehicle positions with status, heading, and last-known location — updated as vehicles move.

Not a subscription routing tool. A platform.

Capability GigaFleet Subscription Routing Tools

✓Bill by project scope, milestones, or contract deliverables — not seat count
–Subscription-only flat fees with no project modeling
SB1383 compliance module

Per-customer white-label
